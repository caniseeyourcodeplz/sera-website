Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Applies to the wakeup. iOS app

The short version: wakeup. has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your alarm, streak, photos and location stay on your iPhone. The only outside service is our subscription provider (RevenueCat), which handles your subscription status. Apple processes all payments.

Who we are

wakeup. is built by Aayan Imran ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: privacy@joinsera.us.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are bought through Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and transaction history, and basic device and app information (such as app version, OS version and IP address). RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf to provide subscription functionality.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up. We don't collect your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs, and no selling or sharing of personal data.

Permissions

iOS asks your permission before the app can use alarms, notifications, location, motion or the camera. You can change any of these anytime in iPhone Settings ▸ wakeup. Some features (like the walk check) won't work without them.

Ratings

After some mornings, the app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. That's handled by Apple; we don't receive your identity.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask about subscription records held by RevenueCat, email privacy@joinsera.us. Manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions.

Children

wakeup. isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
